Overview

The CONGEST model

The best known lower and upper bounds for every problem in the CONGEST model on general graphs, for deterministic and randomized algorithms. As in the LOCAL model, the nodes communicate in synchronous rounds, but every message has only $O(\log n)$ bits. Every lower bound for LOCAL also holds here, while algorithms must work with small messages. Runtimes depend on the number of nodes $n$ and the maximum degree $\Delta$; choose a regime of $\Delta$ to compare them as functions of $n$, or take the worst case over all $\Delta$.

The gap between the bounds is classified as tight (they match asymptotically), almost tight (they match up to a factor polylogarithmic in the lower bound, such as $\log n$ against $\log n / \log\log n$), a polynomial gap (the upper bound is polynomial in the lower bound, such as $\log^{5/3} n$ against $\log n$) or an exponential gap (the upper bound is exponential in the lower bound or more, such as $\mathrm{polylog}\, n$ against $\log^* n$).

Bounds are taken from the results in the database and compared at sample values of Δ, so they can only be as complete as the database. Corrections are welcome on the problem pages.
